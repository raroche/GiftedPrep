## The one-paragraph summary

Florida does not run one gifted test. Rule 6A-6.03019 requires an individually
administered IQ score two standard deviations above the mean (about 130), a
majority of gifted characteristics, and evidence of need. Districts screen for
that individual evaluation with a *group* test, and each picks its own. Across
the twenty largest districts the **NNAT is the most common by far**, grade 2 is
the usual screening year, and the cut score to move on varies from 107 to 122 —
a full standard deviation. Districts with an approved **Plan B** lower the
intellectual bar to about 115 for students who are low-income or English
learners, though Pasco operates no Plan B at all. A parent who does not know
their district's instrument should familiarize their child with all three of the
common group screeners, which is what this app does.
