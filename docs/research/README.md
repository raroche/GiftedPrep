## The one-paragraph summary

Florida does not run one gifted test. Rule 6A-6.03019 requires an individually
administered IQ score two standard deviations above the mean (about 130), a
majority of gifted characteristics, and evidence of need. Districts screen for
that individual evaluation with a *group* test, and they each pick their own:
CogAT in Collier, Raven's 2 in Monroe, NNAT or OLSAT elsewhere. Districts with
an approved **Plan B** lower the intellectual bar to about 115 for students who
are low-income or English learners. So a parent who does not know their
district's instrument should familiarise their child with all three of the
common group screeners, which is what this app does.
